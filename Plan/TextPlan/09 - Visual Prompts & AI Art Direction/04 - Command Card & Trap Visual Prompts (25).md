## 🪤 TRAP FIELD MODELS

> Shared trap props that all five trap cards place on the battlefield (see `TechnicalPlan/04` — 1.2s arming fuse, warning VFX, AoE detonation). One prop set, three states.

**Planted Trap (Hidden State)**
- **Subject:** The shared trap prop nested into a hex tile, invisible to enemy vision until revealed by scouting.
- **Look:** A low dark-violet pressure disc and trigger mechanism barely nestled into the hex grass with a single faint rune glinting; at 64px the silhouette reads as a subtle ground hazard, not a unit.
- **Frame:** Dark Violet (#8A2BE2) frame, skull-and-crossbones icon header.
- **Palette:** Dark Violet + Obsidian Dark.
- **Style:** isometric 2.5D game sprite, stylized fantasy, clean readable silhouette at 64px.
- **Finish:** high-detail, soft golden rim light, game-ready texture, no text.

**Arming Fuse Flash (1.2s Warning State)**
- **Subject:** The 1.2-second arming state when a trap is triggered, flashing a warning before detonation.
- **Look:** The planted trap pulsing with a rapid dark-violet fuse-light strobe, expanding warning rings and tick lines radiating across the hex with a small audio-click ripple icon at the trigger point.
- **Frame:** Dark Violet (#8A2BE2) frame, skull-and-crossbones icon header.
- **Palette:** Dark Violet + Crimson Red.
- **Style:** isometric 2.5D game sprite, stylized fantasy, clean readable silhouette at 64px.
- **Finish:** high-detail, soft golden rim light, game-ready texture, no text.

**Detonation Burst**
- **Subject:** The trap's AoE detonation that damages, slows, roots, blinds, or gasses units on the hex.
- **Look:** A violent violet-and-crimson blast mushrooming from the hex, shrapnel, embers, and hexagonal shockwave rings flattening the grass and scorching the tile in a one-frame game-ready burst.
- **Frame:** Dark Violet (#8A2BE2) frame, skull-and-crossbones icon header.
- **Palette:** Crimson Red + Dark Violet.
