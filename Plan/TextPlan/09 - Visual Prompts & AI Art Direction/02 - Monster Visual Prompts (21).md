## Ironhide Boars

> Physical-power family → drops **Gilded Ore**. Gilded bronze hides, iron plating, and charge-driven aggression.

**Feral Boar**
- **Subject:** A common Ironhide Boar — a young, lean wild boar that roams the jungle fringe as the family's pack-filler.
- **Look:** Stocky, bristled boar with patchy gilded-bronze hide and a few rusted iron plates bolted to its shoulders; the smallest and fastest of the family, about the size of a war hound.
- **Gear/Aura:** A dusty-gold charge glow flaring around its tusks as it knocks back the nearest unit and slows it.
- **Palette:** Gilded bronze hide, warm chestnut bristles, imperial gold tusks, obsidian hooves.
- **Style:** isometric 2.5D game sprite, stylized fantasy, clean readable silhouette at 64px
- **Finish:** high-detail, soft golden rim light, game-ready texture, no text

**Ironhide Boar**
- **Subject:** A rare Ironhide Boar — a veteran war boar, armored and heavier, the mid-tier bruiser of the camp.
- **Look:** Larger, slab-muscled boar sheathed in full iron-and-bronze plate over its gilded hide, scarred snout and gilded tusks; noticeably bulkier than the Feral Boar.
- **Gear/Aura:** A longer charge wind-up with a golden shockwave ring as it smashes through the nearest hex.
- **Palette:** Gilded bronze and polished steel plating, imperial gold tusks, obsidian shadowing.
- **Style:** isometric 2.5D game sprite, stylized fantasy, clean readable silhouette at 64px
- **Finish:** high-detail, soft golden rim light, game-ready texture, no text

**Boar Matriarch**
- **Subject:** An elite Ironhide Boar — the massive alpha sow that leads the lair and drops the family's rarest Gilded Ore.
- **Look:** A colossal sow with layered iron-bronze armor, curled imperial-gold tusks, and a heavy mane of gilded bristles; dwarfs both lower variants.
- **Gear/Aura:** A devastating 2-hex charge leaving a wide golden dust-trail shockwave that knocks back and slows anything in its path.
- **Palette:** Deep bronze armor, imperial gold tusks, obsidian hide, sunburst orange ember-glow at the joints.
- **Style:** isometric 2.5D game sprite, stylized fantasy, clean readable silhouette at 64px
- **Finish:** high-detail, soft golden rim light, game-ready texture, no text
