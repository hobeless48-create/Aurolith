# 🏗️ 05 - FACILITY VISUAL PROMPTS (18)

> Follows `00 - Style Guide & Prompt Template.md` — same 6-line structure, Gilded Empire palette, and global style suffix. 3×3-hex isometric footprint, built T1 → T2 → T3 (T3 = gilded/marble grandeur). Sourced from `Plan/Data/FacilityList/00 - Facility Pool (18 Facilities).md`.

## 🏭 PRODUCTION

**Caravan Post**
- **Subject:** Flagship gold facility — a waystation that pays lump-sum gold every minute when a supply caravan arrives.
- **Look (T1 → T3):** T1 brass-cage caravan dock with a covered wagon and a wooden unloading ramp · T2 stone waystation with twin trade signs flanking the road and a paved cart court · T3 gilded toll-gate courtyard with a permanently arriving merchant train of gilded wagons streaming in.
- **Signature:** The covered wagon pulling into the dock on the minute boundary.
- **Palette:** Imperial Gold `#D4AF37` + brass/wood (T1) → stone (T2) → marble & gilt (T3).
- **Style:** isometric 2.5D game sprite, stylized fantasy, clean readable silhouette at 64px
- **Finish:** high-detail, soft golden rim light, game-ready texture, no text

**Hunter's Lodge**

- **Palette:** Imperial Gold `#D4AF37` + wood & Jungle Bronze `#CD7F32` (T1) → stone (T2) → marble & gilt (T3).
- **Style:** isometric 2.5D game sprite, stylized fantasy, clean readable silhouette at 64px
- **Finish:** high-detail, soft golden rim light, game-ready texture, no text
