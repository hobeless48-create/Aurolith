# 👑 THE GILDED EMPIRE: WORLD & LORE SPECIFICATION

> [!NOTE]
> High-fantasy lore setting, visual aesthetic guidelines, and military commander identity for **AUROLITH** (formerly General Gilded).

---

## 🏛️ World Overview & Aesthetic Tone

- **Setting:** **The High Empire of Aurum & The Ancient Auroliths** — a world of gold-infused architecture, alchemical war tech, sun-blessed fortresses, and mythical monoliths (*Auroliths*) anchoring the tactical hex ley-lines.
- **Visual Aesthetic:**
  - **Color Palette:** Deep Imperial Gold (`#D4AF37`), Royal Crimson (`#990000`), Obsidian Black (`#111111`), and Sun-Gilded Marble (`#F5F5DC`).
  - **Architecture:** Towering golden ramparts, intricate brass gears, alchemical mintage crucibles, and radiant siege obelisks.
  - **Armaments:** Sun-forged steel plate armor, gold-embroidered banners, enchanted heavy arbalests, and alchemically powered siege mortar batteries.

---

## 🎖️ The Commander Role

- Players step into the boots of **High Imperial Commanders** directing multi-platoon battle plans from elevated tactical command platforms.
- Rather than controlling a single hero avatar on the field, Commanders orchestrate the entire war machine: drafting traits, managing inner wall facility slot economies, issuing card commands, and drawing platoon path vectors across the 90×150 hex board.

---

## 🌿 The Jungle Frontier (Wild Aurum)

- Flanking the central imperial battlefield are the **Wild Jungle Zones**.
- Teeming with ancient beasts, alchemical flora, and neutral monster camps, the Jungle represents raw resource potential.
- Harvesting the jungle yields gold and XP, but imperial generals who wander too far into the wilderness risk having their golden castles breached from behind.
