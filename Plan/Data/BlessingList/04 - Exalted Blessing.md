# ✦ TIER 04 — EXALTED (30 Blessings)

> **Moderate.** Solid, always-usable value with a tactical hook. **Design rule: no flat-stat-only traits — every trait is a 2-line hook.** Draft & pool rules: `00 - Blessing System Overview, Draft Rules & Audit.md`.
> **Structure:** 10 Combat · 10 Economy · 10 Item.

---

## ⚔️ Combat — 10

### 1. Iron Constitution
- All units gain **+5% Max HP**. Units **above 90% HP** deal **+5% damage**.
- *Healthy armies hit harder — keep platoons topped to cash in.*

### 2. Weapon Drills
- All units gain **+6% ATK**. Each unit's **first attack of a fight** deals **+50% damage**.
- *Every unit opens fights with a haymaker.*

### 3. Heavy Plating
- All units gain **+8 Armor**. Taking a **melee hit** grants **+5% damage reduction for 3s** (stacking).
- *The longer the front line brawls, the tougher it gets.*

### 4. Warding Sigils
- All units gain **+8 Magic Resist**. Taking an **ability hit** grants **+5% Magic Resist for 3s** (stacking).
- *Every spell thrown at you makes your magic defense climb.*

### 5. War Drums
- Your **first platoon** gains **+8% Attack Speed** and **+5% move speed**. It deals **+10% damage** to the first enemy platoon it engages each fight.
- *Your main push platoon opens every fight hot.*

### 6. Battlefield Medics
- All units recover **1% missing HP per second**. Below **50% HP**, this **doubles to 2%**.
- *The worse things look, the faster the medics work.*

### 7. Shield Wall
- Melee units gain **+6% damage** and **+6 Armor**. Melee units **within 1 hex of a melee ally** gain **+5% damage reduction**.
- *Front lines that stand shoulder-to-shoulder hold harder.*

### 8. Suppressing Fire
- Ranged units gain **+6% Attack Speed**. Their attacks **slow the target by 8% for 2s** (stacking).
- *Backlines turn into slow-firing nets — nothing escapes the barrage.*

### 9. Rams & Siege Towers
- All units deal **+8% siege damage** vs walls. **Damaging a wall** grants the attacker **+5% lifesteal for 5s**.
- *Siege work sustains the army — pounding walls keeps your units healthy.*

### 10. Martyr's Resolve
- **Casualty Counter-Play (Q3 Synergy):** Whenever a friendly unit dies, all surviving units in that platoon gain **+15% Tenacity** (CC duration reduction) and recover **10% of their Max HP over 4s**.
- *Turn focus-fire against the enemy — taking down your vanguard only strengthens the remaining survivors.*

## 🪙 Economy — 10

### 1. Holy Nation *(cross-tier: Ordinary +10% · Extra +20% · Perfect +40%)*
- Permanently gain **+20% production rate** (all passive gold, XP, and Arsenal material generation).
- *The mid-tier of the lineage — unconditional and universal.*

### 2. Gilded Vault
- **+8% passive gold**. Saving past each **new 500-gold threshold** grants a **one-time +50 gold bonus**.
- *Every bank milestone tips you — saving literally pays.*

### 3. War Chest
- Start with **+120 gold**. If you **spend it all within the first minute**, gain **+30 bonus gold**.
- *An early-tempo bet — invest fast, get a kickback.*

### 4. Merchant's Bargain
- Recruit costs **−8%**. **Every 5th recruit is free**.
- *Cheaper army, and a freebie on the roster.*

### 5. Bounty Hunter
- **+15% gold & XP bounties** from jungle camps. Each camp you clear gains **1 charge** — at **5 charges**, gain **+50 gold**.
- *Farm steadily and the camps start paying out milestone bonuses.*

### 6. Scholar's Guild
- **+15% passive XP**. Every **2 levels gained**, all units gain **+1% Max HP permanently**.
- *Teching up slowly trains the army.*

### 7. Frugal Engineer
- Craft & upgrade costs **−8%**. **Every 8th craft or upgrade is free**.
- *The build pipeline that regularly gives one back.*

### 8. Silver Interest
- Every minute: **+2 gold per 100 saved** (cap **+50/min**). The cap **rises by +10 for every 1,000 gold of bank**.
- *The bigger the war chest, the higher the dividend ceiling.*

### 9. Spoils of War
- Kills grant **+3 gold** (cap 15 per platoon engagement). **Every 10th kill** grants **+50 gold**.
- *Combat pays in coins, and every milestone kill pays out.*

### 10. Courier Line
- Every **2 minutes**: **+60 gold**. If you have a **Scout active** when it ticks, **+30 extra gold**.
- *Scouting is its own paycheck — the 10s scout window pays double.*

## 🧪 Item — 10

### 1. Field Medic Cache
- Start with **3 Healing Flasks** and **1 Damage Tonic**. Consumables from this cache heal **10% more**.
- *A ready sustain-and-damage kit that's a little stronger than store-bought.*

### 2. Journeyman Tools
- Craft time **−20%**, craft gold **−8%**. **Every 5th craft is free**.
- *Faster, cheaper crafts — with a freebie on the pipeline.*

### 3. Tempering
- Item stat bonuses **+10%**. **Equipping an item** grants the unit **+3% Max HP** (once per unit).
- *Bigger items, and gearing up toughens the wearer.*

### 4. Alchemist's Touch
- Consumable durations **+40%**. Using a consumable grants **+5% Attack Speed** for its duration.
- *Potions last longer and let the carrier swing faster.*

### 5. Salvage Expert
- Salvaging refunds **+20% gold** (above the 30% baseline). **Salvaging 3 items** grants **1 free Common material**.
- *Recycling pays — and pays out a material now and then.*

### 6. Provision Cache
- Start with **1 Rare material** of your choice. **Crafting with it** grants **+10% XP for 30s**.
- *A Rare head start that also feeds your tech.*

### 7. Issue Equipment
- Your **starter platoon** starts with **1 free Rare-quality item**. Its carrier gains **+4% move speed**.
- *The starter platoon is pre-geared and a little faster.*

### 8. Bazaar Contract
- Bazaar pity prices **−15%**. Buying **3 materials in a turn** makes the **4th free**.
- *The fallback lane is cheaper and rewards bulk buying.*

### 9. Assembly Line
- Every craft has a **15% chance** to refund a component material. **Every 5th craft** is **50% faster**.
- *The crafting loop that feeds itself in materials and speed.*

### 10. Supply Runner
- Every **3 minutes**: **1 free Rare material**. While you hold **3+ Rare materials**, production rate **+5%**.
- *A steady Rare drip that pays a production bonus for stockpiling.*
