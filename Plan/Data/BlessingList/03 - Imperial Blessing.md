# ✦ TIER 03 — IMPERIAL (30 Blessings)

> **Strong · synergetic.** The legacy blessing pool lives here in upgraded, hook-driven form. **Design rule: no flat-stat-only traits — every trait is a 2-line hook.** Draft & pool rules: `00 - Blessing System Overview, Draft Rules & Audit.md`.
> **Structure:** 10 Combat · 10 Economy · 10 Item.

---

## ⚔️ Combat — 10

### 1. Iron Legion
- All units gain **+8% Max HP** and **+8 Armor**. Units that have **taken damage within the last 5s** gain **+10% damage reduction**.
- *The more the enemy commits, the tankier you get — frontline pressure feeds its own counter.*

### 2. Vanguard Strike
- All units deal **+10% physical damage**. When a platoon **first strikes an unbreached wall segment**, it gains **+20% siege damage for 10s** and heals **10% of its Max HP**.
- *First contact with the wall pays double — damage and a heal to keep pushing.*

### 3. Golden Fervor
- Kills grant the platoon **+5% Attack Speed for 8s** (max **+25%**). At **max stacks**, the platoon's **next attack is a guaranteed crit for 200%**.
- *Every kill builds toward a guaranteed big hit — snowball fights you're winning.*

### 4. Bloodlust
- **12% melee lifesteal** on physical attacks. Below **30% HP**, attack speed **doubles for 5s** and lifesteal **triples**.
- *The classic enrage — the closer to death, the faster and greedier the front line gets.*

### 5. Precision Aim
- Arbalest/Marksmen gain **+1 range** and **15% crit chance (×1.8)**. **Crits pierce** to a 2nd target at **50% damage**.
- *Backline crits that hit two targets — cleave pressure from the back row.*

### 6. Iron Armor
- Infantry & Siege units gain **+25 Armor** and **+50 Max HP**. **Above 60% HP** they gain **+10% damage reduction**.
- *The wall classes hold — and hold best while healthy.*

### 7. Adrenaline Rush
- Below **30% HP**, units gain **+15% damage** and **+15% damage reduction**. Entering this state grants **+30% move speed for 3s**.
- *Wounded units bite back — and scramble into position to do it.*

### 8. Phoenix Protocol
- The **first unit to die each fight revives at 40% HP**. On revival it **explodes for 100% of its ATK** as damage to nearby enemies.
- *A free life that also punishes whoever stacked on the corpse.*

### 9. Fortress Mind
- All units gain **+15% CC-duration resist**. Platoons **defending your own wall** take **10% less damage**; when your wall is **breached**, all units gain **+15% damage for 10s**.
- *The defender's trait — losing a wall only makes your army angrier.*

### 10. Titan's Grip
- Melee units gain **+12% damage** and **+10% Max HP**. After each kill they gain **+4% damage** (stacks to **+20%**, persists for the fight).
- *Melee snowball — each front-line kill makes the next one hit harder.*

## 🪙 Economy — 10

### 1. Holy Nation *(cross-tier: Ordinary +10% · Extra +20% · Perfect +40%)*
- Permanently gain **+40% production rate** (all passive gold, XP, and Arsenal material generation).
- *The unconditional universal engine — strongest tier of the lineage.*

### 2. Gilded Taxes
- **+15% passive gold**. Breaching a wall grants **+100 gold** and **doubles camp bounties for 20s**.
- *Taxes on everything, and walls open a short double-income window.*

### 3. Royal Treasury
- Start with **+250 gold**. While your bank is **above 1,500 gold**, recruits cost **10% less**.
- *Rich start, richer army — hoarding keeps paying discounts.*

### 4. Merchant Discount
- Recruit costs **−15%**; card-buy scaling caps at **150 gold**. **Every 3rd card you buy is free**.
- *Cheaper army, capped cards, and a free draw every third buy.*

### 5. Rapid Bounty
- **+30% gold & XP bounties** from jungle camps. Clearing an **Elite lair** grants **1 free Rare material**.
- *Farm faster, and the big camps tip in extra gear.*

### 6. Scholar's Blessing *(legacy, upgraded)*
- **+25% passive XP**; facility upgrade costs **−15%**. **Maxing a facility** grants **+50 gold**.
- *Teching up pays — every finished building refunds part of itself.*

### 7. Master Builder
- Craft & upgrade costs **−15%**; craft time **−30%**. **Every 10th craft or upgrade is free**.
- *The build pipeline that occasionally gives you one back.*

### 8. Golden Interest
- Every minute: **+2 gold per 100 saved** (cap **+60/min**). Ending the minute **above 1,000 gold** grants **+30 bonus gold**.
- *The hoarder dividend with a milestone tip.*

### 9. War Economy
- Kills grant **+5 gold**, monsters **+8 gold**. **10 kills within a single minute** grants **+100 bonus gold**.
- *The blood economy — fight fast, get paid faster.*

### 10. Vault Speculation
- **Facility Gamble (Q2 Confirmed):** Lock **500 Gold** into your Golden Vault for **3:00 minutes** (funds are frozen). If the Golden Vault remains standing at the 3-minute mark, it yields **1,250 Gold** (+750g profit). If the Golden Vault is demolished by attackers before 3:00, all **500 Gold is permanently lost** (Zero safety net).
- *Courtyard stakes — place a huge bounty in your base and dare the enemy to come demolish it.*

## 🧪 Item — 10

### 1. Alchemical Cache
- Start with **3 Healing Flasks** and **1 Siege Bomb**. The Siege Bomb deals **+15% damage**.
- *Instant sustain and a heavier opening siege threat.*

### 2. Master Smith
- Item stat bonuses **+30%**. **Equipping an item** heals the unit for **8% of its Max HP**.
- *Bigger items, and every equip is a top-up.*

### 3. Elixir Mastery
- Consumable durations **doubled**. Consumed potions also grant **5% lifesteal** for their duration.
- *Longer buffs that also let the carrier fight-back-heal.*

### 4. Relic Affinity
- Designate **1 platoon**: its units may each equip a **2nd item**. That platoon gains **+5% damage per item equipped**.
- *Arm your carry platoon twice — and every piece of gear on it adds damage.*

### 5. Quick Crafting
- Craft time **−50%**; craft gold **−25%**. The **first craft after each camp clear** is **30% faster**.
- *The fastest pipeline — and camp clears keep the tempo going.*

### 6. Pandora's Forge
- **Once per turn**, transform 1 item into a **random** item of the same quality. **Transforming 3 items** grants **1 free Common material**.
- *A slot-machine reroll every turn that slowly pays materials back.*

### 7. Salvage King
- Salvaging refunds **+30% gold** and has a **50% chance** to also refund the component materials. Each salvage grants **+5% craft speed for 1 minute**.
- *Rebuild loadouts without losing the farm — and craft faster while doing it.*

### 8. Arsenal Cache
- Start with **1 Epic material** of your choice + **1 Siege Bomb**. Crafting with the Epic material grants the item's carrier **+5% Max HP**.
- *An Epic at minute zero, with a bonus for whoever wields the result.*

### 9. Standard Issue
- Your **starter platoon** begins with **1 free Epic-quality item**. That item's carrier gains **+5% Max HP** and **+5% damage**.
- *The carry platoon starts pre-geared and pre-buffed.*

### 10. Bazaar Patron
- Bazaar pity prices **−25%**; stock refreshes free. The **first Bazaar buy each minute** is **10% cheaper**.
- *The fallback lane is cheap, stocked, and discounts itself every minute.*
