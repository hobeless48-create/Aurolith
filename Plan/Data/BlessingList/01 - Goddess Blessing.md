# ✦ TIER 01 — GODDESS (30 Blessings)

> **Tier ceiling.** Every pick is a build-around — a single Goddess blessing defines how your run plays. **Design rule: no flat-stat-only traits — every trait is a 2-line hook** (main effect + trigger/payoff). Draft & pool rules: `00 - Blessing System Overview, Draft Rules & Audit.md`.
> **Structure:** 10 Combat · 10 Economy · 10 Item.

---

## ⚔️ Combat — 10

### 1. Unbreakable
- All your units gain **+40% Max HP** and **+20% Armor & Magic Resist** (final, after all other bonuses).
- *The only number this trait respects is theirs — opponents must draft anti-tank (Breaker, executes, God's Wrath) to answer it.*

### 2. Avatars of War
- All units gain **+20% ATK**. Whenever a unit lands a killing blow, it releases a **shockwave** dealing **15% of its ATK as true damage** to nearby enemies.
- *Every kill pays into the next kill — an army that wins fights, wins them faster.*

### 3. The Undying Host
- Every unit revives **once per fight at 45% HP** on its first death, then deals **30% of its ATK as damage to nearby enemies**.
- *The "no, I don't think I will die" trait — a free life that also punishes whoever stacked on the corpse.*

### 4. Eternal Bloodlust
- **20% lifesteal** on all damage dealt. Below **40% HP**, units gain **+40% damage and +40% Attack Speed**.
- *The wounded army is its deadliest version — sustain and rage in one.*

### 5. God's Wrath
- Every **15s**, a meteor strikes the **highest-HP enemy platoon** for **20% of its total Max HP as true damage**.
- *Free, unavoidable, scaling damage that ignores armor — hard-counters stall comps and Elite lairs.*

### 6. Overlord
- Your **highest-value unit** gains **+50% to all stats**. The platoon it leads gains **+20% damage**.
- *A single crowned carry that drags its whole platoon up with it — synergy with your 5-start pool core.*

### 7. Timeless
- All ability cooldowns tick **30% faster**. Every time a unit casts an ability, it gains **+3% damage for 5s** (stacks, refreshed on each cast).
- *The more they cast, the harder they hit — spell-heavy comps go nuclear.*

### 8. Apex Predator
- All units deal **+30% damage vs monsters**; jungle monsters drop **1 extra material copy** per kill.
- *The jungle power fantasy — your farm feeds the item pipeline while combat power stays high.*

### 9. Wallcrusher
- All units deal **+35% siege damage** vs enemy walls. **Each wall segment you destroy** permanently grants all units **+4% damage** (max **+20%**).
- **Divine condition:** destroying an enemy wall segment for the **first time** grants **1 random Divine item** (`Data/ItemList/00` §5.5).
- *Break walls → the whole army snowballs — structure damage that compounds into army power.*

### 10. Commander of the Five
- Your **5 starter units** each gain **+30% Max HP and +20% damage**; platoons they are on gain **+10% CDR**.
- *Crowns the army you actually built at match start — the 5-start pool becomes your signature squad.*

## 🪙 Economy — 10

### 1. Gilded Empire
- **+40% passive gold** income. Whenever your army **wipes an enemy platoon**, gold income is **tripled for 10s**.
- *The engine that rewards aggression — every wipe opens a short cash-printing window.*

### 2. Endless Treasury
- Every minute: **+250 gold + 6% of your current gold bank**. The first time you reach **2,000 bank**, gain **1 free Epic item**.
- **Divine condition:** at **5,000 bank**, gain **1 random Divine item** (`Data/ItemList/00` §5.5).
- *The exponential printer — the earlier you draft it, the harder it snowballs.*

### 3. Bounty Empire
- **+75% gold & XP bounties** from jungle camps. **Elite-lair bosses** drop **double material copies**.
- **Divine condition:** after clearing **10 jungle camps**, gain **1 random Divine item** (`Data/ItemList/00` §5.5).
- *Jungle time converts into both resources and gear — the farm-to-fight converter.*

### 4. Omniscient Academy
- **+50% passive XP**; facility upgrade costs **−50%**. Every time you max a facility, **all facilities gain +5% efficiency permanently**.
- *Tech snowball — each finished building makes every other building stronger.*

### 5. Monopoly
- Unit recruit costs **−40%**; card-buy scaling caps at **50 gold**. **Every 5th recruit is free and arrives instantly on the field**.
- *The mass-army pick — flood the board before opponents can react.*

### 6. Golden Age
- **All gold gains ×1.5**. Spending **500 gold within a single minute** grants **1 free Card Draw**.
- *The greed dream — spend hard and the game hands you cards back.*

### 7. Master of Coin
- Crafting and upgrades cost **0 gold**. Gold you would have spent is **stored as a War Fund** — every **1,000 stored** instantly pays **+300 gold**.
- *The gold sink is gone — and the savings pay you back.*

### 8. The Gilded Chrysalis
- **Hard Cashout Gamble (Q2 Confirmed):** Banks all passive gold income for **4:00 minutes** (accumulated gold is locked and unavailable for spending). At the **4:00 cashout**, grants **2.5× banked gold** + **2 Random Divine Items**. If **Platoon 1 is completely wiped** before 4:00, the cashout is **permanently forfeited** (Zero safety net).
- *Extreme risk, monumental payoff — sacrifice the early-game economy to fund a catastrophic late-game siege.*

### 9. Golden Millennium
- At **10:00**: **+1,500 gold**, **1 free Epic item**, and **1 random Divine item** (`Data/ItemList/00` §5.5). Every camp you clear before 10:00 refunds **10% of its bounty** as bonus gold.
- *A scheduled explosion that also rewards how hard you farmed to reach it.*

### 10. Eternal Growth
- Production rate **+3% per minute** (max **+60%**). Each minute-milestone you hit with a kill that minute grants **+50 bonus gold**.
- *Late-game scaling where every minute of the match counts retroactively.*

## 🧪 Item — 10

### 1. Relic of the First Empire
- Start with a **Transcendence-unique artifact** (1 item slot; cannot be crafted/combined/salvaged): the carrier gains **+20% all stats** and its platoon gains **+10% damage reduction**.
- *A free build-around item that also patches its own platoon.*

### 2. Celestial Forge
- Craft time **−80%**, craft gold **−50%**. Every craft has a **40% chance to come out one quality tier higher** (quality-upgrade caps at **Ancient** — never Divine).
- **Divine condition:** your **10th craft** of the match comes out as **1 random Divine item** (`Data/ItemList/00` §5.5).
- *Near-instant crafting with constant free quality upgrades.*

### 3. Pandora's Genesis
- **Once per turn**, upgrade any 1 item **one quality tier** — or transform it into **any** item of the same quality (your choice).
- *Ultimate item control — perfect builds every game.*

### 4. Armory Eternal
- All units may equip **2 items**. Every **3 minutes**, **one equipped item gains +5% stats permanently** (stacking).
- *Doubles the item system's footprint — and gear keeps growing on its own.*

### 5. Apex Artificer
- Item stat bonuses **+75%**. Consumable effects **last until the unit dies** instead of expiring.
- *Every equip is a permanent power spike; every potion is a permanent buff.*

### 6. Reliquary
- Start with **5 random items** (one of each quality incl. 1 Epic) + **2 Siege Bombs** + **1 Epic material**.
- *The biggest starting loadout in the game — full board strength before the first craft.*

### 7. War Engine
- Every platoon starts with **1 free Common item**. Every **2 enemy unit kills** instantly crafts **1 free Common item** to the Arsenal.
- **Divine condition:** every **6 enemy unit kills** additionally crafts **1 random Divine item** (`Data/ItemList/00` §5.5).
- *The army that equips itself — kills literally produce gear.*

### 8. Perfect Understanding
- Salvaging refunds **full component materials** and may be done **anywhere**. **+1 salvage** available per turn.
- *Lossless recycling — rebuild your whole loadout mid-match for free.*

### 9. The Creator
- **Once per match**, craft any item **instantly and free** (bypasses materials and table) — **and receive a free duplicate copy**.
- *Two perfect items on demand — the ultimate late-game flex.*

### 10. Grand Bazaar
- Bazaar pity prices **−75%**; unlimited purchases; free refreshes. The Bazaar also **sells full Common items**.
- *Money converts directly into gear — the Bazaar becomes a real supply line.*
