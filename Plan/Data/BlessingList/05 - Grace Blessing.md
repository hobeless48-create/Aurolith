# ✦ TIER 05 — GRACE (30 Blessings)

> **Small · consistent.** Safe filler that every comp can take — but even the entry tier gets a tactical hook. **Design rule: no flat-stat-only traits — every trait is a 2-line hook.** Draft & pool rules: `00 - Blessing System Overview, Draft Rules & Audit.md`.
> **Structure:** 10 Combat · 10 Economy · 10 Item.

---

## ⚔️ Combat — 10

### 1. Fitness Regimen
- All units gain **+3% Max HP**. Your wall gains **+5% Max HP**.
- *A small army boost that also hardens your defensive line.*

### 2. Training Grounds
- All units gain **+4% ATK**. The **first 3 kills** of the match each grant **+1% ATK permanently**.
- *Early kills make the whole army a little stronger for the rest of the game.*

### 3. Padded Jackets
- All units gain **+5 Armor**. Each unit's **first melee hit received per fight** deals **5% reduced damage**.
- *A little armor, and the opening blow lands softer.*

### 4. Warding Chalk
- All units gain **+5 Magic Resist**. Each unit's **first ability hit received per fight** deals **5% reduced damage**.
- *A little MR, and the first spell lands softer.*

### 5. Drill Sergeant
- Your **first platoon** gains **+6% Attack Speed**. It opens every fight with **10% more damage on its first 3 attacks**.
- *Your main push platoon starts every fight ahead.*

### 6. Mess Hall
- All units recover **0.5% missing HP per second**. Units **out of combat** (no damage for 10s) recover **1.5% missing HP per second**.
- *Rest between fights is where the army really heals.*

### 7. Shield Drills
- Melee units gain **+4% damage**. Each kill grants them **+2% damage permanently** (max **+10%**).
- *Front-line kills leave a lasting mark — snowball in the small tier.*

### 8. Quick Hands
- Ranged units gain **+4% Attack Speed**. Each unit's **first attack of a fight** is a **guaranteed crit for 150%**.
- *Backlines open every fight with a guaranteed big hit.*

### 9. Ladders & Hooks
- All units deal **+4% siege damage** vs walls. The **first 5 wall hits each fight** deal **double damage**.
- *Every fight opens with a burst of structure damage.*

### 10. Grit
- Below **30% HP**, units gain **+6% damage reduction**. Entering this state grants **+10% damage for 3s**.
- *Wounded units hold on — and bite back once.*

## 🪙 Economy — 10

### 1. Holy Nation *(cross-tier: Ordinary +10% · Extra +20% · Perfect +40%)*
- Permanently gain **+10% production rate** (all passive gold, XP, and Arsenal material generation).
- *The weakest tier of the lineage — but unconditional and universal.*

### 2. Gilded Ledger
- **+5% passive gold**. Saving past each **new 300-gold threshold** grants **+25 gold**.
- *Every bank milestone tips you a little.*

### 3. War Chest — Small
- Start with **+75 gold**. Spending it on recruits refunds **+10 gold per recruit** (first 3 recruits).
- *An opening bank that gives a little back on your first recruits.*

### 4. Haggler's Eye
- Recruit costs **−5%**. The **first recruit each minute** costs **50 gold less** (min 50).
- *A small discount, plus a cheap recruit every minute.*

### 5. Bounty Trail
- **+10% gold & XP bounties** from jungle camps. Your **first camp clear** each game grants **1 bonus Common material**.
- *The jungle pays a little more — and the first camp tips in gear.*

### 6. Night School
- **+10% passive XP**. Each time you **level up**, your **next facility upgrade is 10% cheaper** (stacks).
- *Teching up makes the next build cheaper.*

### 7. Penny Pincher
- Craft & upgrade costs **−5%**. **Every 10th craft or upgrade is free**.
- *The build pipeline that slowly gives one back.*

### 8. Copper Interest
- Every minute: **+1 gold per 100 saved** (cap **+30/min**). Saving **1,000 gold** **doubles the cap**.
- *The tiniest hoarder dividend — but it can grow.*

### 9. Scrap Pickers
- Monster kills grant **+5 gold**. Clearing a camp of **5+ monsters** grants **+15 bonus gold**.
- *Big camp clears pay a little extra.*

### 10. Courier Contract
- Every **2 minutes**: **+40 gold**. Each **Scout mission** you run grants **+10 gold** when it resolves.
- *Scouting is a small paycheck — intel that funds itself.*

## 🧪 Item — 10

### 1. Field Rations
- Start with **2 Healing Flasks**. These flasks heal **10% more** than normal.
- *A small sustain kit that works a little better than store-bought.*

### 2. Apprentice Tools
- Craft time **−12%**; craft gold **−5%**. Your **first craft each game** is **50% faster**.
- *A slow-but-steady pipeline with a fast opening craft.*

### 3. Wax & Oil
- Item stat bonuses **+5%**. **Equipping an item** grants the unit **+2% Max HP** (once per unit).
- *Bigger items, and gearing up slightly toughens the wearer.*

### 4. Long Shelf Life
- Consumable durations **+20%**. Consumed potions also grant **+3% move speed** while active.
- *Potions last longer and let the carrier reposition faster.*

### 5. Salvage Scrap
- Salvaging refunds **+10% gold** (above the 30% baseline). Each salvage grants **+5% craft speed for 30s**.
- *Recycling pays a little — and speeds up your next craft.*

### 6. Starter Cache
- Start with **1 Common material** of your choice. **Crafting with it** grants **+5% XP for 30s**.
- *One free component that also feeds your tech.*

### 7. Expedition Gear
- Your **starter platoon** starts with **1 free Common-quality item**. Its carrier gains **+3% Max HP**.
- *The starter platoon is pre-geared and slightly tougher.*

### 8. Bazaar Voucher
- Your **first Bazaar buy** is **20% off**. **Every 4th Bazaar buy** grants **1 free Common material**.
- *The fallback lane gives you a discount and a freebie.*

### 9. Batch Order
- Every craft has a **10% chance** to refund a random Common material. **Every 6th craft is free**.
- *The crafting loop that occasionally pays for itself.*

### 10. Assistant's Loan
- Every **3 minutes**: **1 free Common material**. **Crafting within 30s** of receiving it grants **+5% craft speed for 1 minute**.
- *A steady drip that rewards using it right away.*
