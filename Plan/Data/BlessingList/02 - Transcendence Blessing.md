# ✦ TIER 02 — TRANSCENDENCE (30 Blessings)

> **Powerful · unique.** Signature mechanics, unique items, strong build hooks. **Design rule: no flat-stat-only traits — every trait is a 2-line hook.** Draft & pool rules: `00 - Blessing System Overview, Draft Rules & Audit.md`.
> **Structure:** 10 Combat · 10 Economy · 10 Item.

---

## ⚔️ Combat — 10

### 1. Commander
- **Randomly select one unit from your 5-start pool.** It gains **×2 Max HP**. Any platoon this unit is on: all units gain **+20% Cooldown Reduction** and **2% Lost-HP recovery per second** while it survives.
- *Every match crowns a different starter — keep the champion alive and the whole platoon becomes immortal.*

### 2. Immortal Vanguard
- All units gain **+12% Max HP** and **+12 Armor & Magic Resist**. Units **above 90% HP** take **10% reduced damage**.
- *Full-health armies get tankier the healthier they are — keep platoons topped to cash in.*

### 3. Legion's Wrath
- All units gain **+15% ATK**. Each kill grants the killer **+6% Attack Speed for 8s** (stacks to **+30%**) — at **max stacks**, the next kill heals the killer for **20% of its Max HP**.
- *Kill-feed tempo that tops off — the more you win fights, the better you sustain them.*

### 4. Soul Siphon
- **18% lifesteal** on all damage. Below **30% HP**, units deal **+40% damage** and **lifesteal doubles**.
- *The danger-zone trait — bleeding units both hurt and heal harder.*

### 5. Apex Marksman
- Ranged units gain **+1 attack range** and **20% crit chance (×2.0)**. **Crits grant +10% move speed for 3s**.
- *Backline carries kite harder after every big hit.*

### 6. Adamantine
- Melee units gain **+20% damage reduction** and **+15% Max HP**. The **first attack** each melee unit receives per fight deals **30% reduced damage**.
- *The front line survives the opening burst and keeps on walling.*

### 7. Breaker
- Every attack shreds the target's **Armor & Magic Resist by 3** (max 15, 5s). **Enemies at 15 shred stacks are stunned for 1s**.
- *Stack shreds into a stun — attack-speed comps turn into crowd control.*

### 8. Time Warp
- All ability cooldowns tick **20% faster**. Every **10s**, **one random friendly unit's lowest-cooldown ability becomes instantly ready**.
- *Abilities come up faster AND one lucky cast fires free every 10 seconds.*

### 9. Undying Legion
- The **first 2 units to die each fight revive at 50% HP**. On revival they gain **+20% damage for 5s**.
- *Two free bodies that come back angry.*

### 10. Last Bastion Vow
- **Fortress Breach Comeback (Q3 Synergy):** If your Castle Outer Wall is breached, all friendly units gain **+25% Damage Reduction** and **+20% Attack Speed** within your courtyard and the central battlefield for **3:00 minutes**.
- *The siege inverter — an opponent who punches through your wall faces an entrenched, frenzied defense.*

## 🪙 Economy — 10

### 1. Gilded Monarchy
- **+25% passive gold**; **+150 gold** per enemy wall breach. After each breach, **camp bounties are doubled for 20s**.
- *Breach → cash AND a farm window — aggressive pushes pay twice.*

### 2. War Bonds
- Every minute: **+50 gold + 3% of your current gold bank**. While your bank is **above 3,000 gold**, recruits cost **10% less**.
- *Hoarders get richer and recruit cheaper.*

### 3. Bounty Cartel
- **+50% gold & XP bounties** from jungle camps. The **first clear of each monster family** each game grants **1 extra Rare material**.
- *Farm the variety — each family's debut pays a Rare bonus.*

### 4. Academy of Genius
- **+40% passive XP**; facility upgrade costs **−30%**. Every time you **level up**, all units gain **+1% Max HP permanently**.
- *Teching up literally trains your army.*

### 5. Tycoon
- Recruit costs **−25%**; card-buy scaling caps at **100 gold**. **Every 10th recruit is free and arrives with a free Common item**.
- *Mass recruiting that eventually equips itself.*

### 6. Midas Touch
- **All gold gains ×1.25**. Every minute, if you **out-earned your opponent** that minute, gain **+25 bonus gold**.
- *The gold race amplifier — winning the income race pays a bonus every minute.*

### 7. Forge Baron
- Craft & upgrade costs **−25%**. Every craft refunds **20% of its gold cost as a material** from that craft's component families.
- *The crafting economy — spend gold, get materials back.*

### 8. Royal Charter
- You may construct the **Imperial Gate** — a unique facility found nowhere else in the pool — in one of your 6 Inner Wall Slots (counts as a slot; its own **Unique** category; cannot be duplicated). **Imperial Gate:** T1 — platoon teleport **Core ↔ Gate**, CD **60s** · T2 — CD **45s**, units hired while the Gate is your commander target **spawn at the Gate** · T3 — CD **30s**, teleported platoons gain **+20% damage for 5s**. Standard 500/750/1000 HP & 150g / 250g+100XP / 400g+250XP costs.
- *One exclusive landmark, one slot — the only army in the game that can be in two places at once.*

### 9. Golden Hour
- At **10:00**: **+800 gold**. Every camp you clear before 10:00 adds **+25 gold** to the windfall.
- *A scheduled spike that scales with how hard you farmed the opening.*

### 10. Infinite Growth
- Production rate **+2% per minute** (max **+30%**). Your **material drip** grows at the same rate.
- *A dual engine — resources AND gear generation both scale as the match runs long.*

## 🧪 Item — 10

> **Ancient gate:** Genius is the **lowest tier that can produce Ancient items** (`Data/ItemList/00` §4 — 2.0× stats + 2.0× effect). Quality-upgrade caps at **Ancient** and can never produce a **Divine** item (explicit Goddess blessing conditions only, ItemList §5.5).

### 1. Doom's Staff
- Gain the **unique item Doom's Staff** (1 item slot; cannot be crafted/combined/salvaged): grants the carrier **+AP / magic attack stats**. **On hit vs an enemy**, both the carrier and target have **Armor & Magic Resist halved for 5s**.
- *The wall's 70% damage reduction is NOT affected by the debuff — a double-edged shredder.*

### 2. Pandora's Bench
- **Once per turn**, transform any 1 item into **any** item of the **same quality** (your choice).
- *Full item freedom — no wasted crafts, perfect builds every game.*

### 3. Grand Armory
- All units may equip a **2nd item**. The first time each unit equips its 2nd item, it gains **+5% Max HP permanently**.
- *Double gear, and the act of gearing up makes the army tougher.*

### 4. Grand Forge
- Craft time **−60%**, craft gold **−35%**. Every craft has a **20% chance to come out one quality tier higher** (may roll **Ancient** — Genius+ gate).
- *Fast, cheap, quality-boosting crafts — a steady stream of Rare+ gear.*

### 5. Artisan's Blessing
- Item stat bonuses **+40%**; consumable durations **doubled**. Consuming a potion also heals the unit for **8% Max HP**.
- *Bigger items, longer potions, and instant sustain on every sip.*

### 6. Relic Hoard
- Start with **3 random items** (Common, Rare, Epic) + **2 Siege Bombs**. Items you start with grant their carriers **+3% Max HP**.
- *A ready-made loadout that also toughens up its holders.*

### 7. War Machinery
- Every platoon starts with **1 free Common item**. Every **3 enemy unit kills** charges **1 free Siege Bomb**.
- *Army-wide gear plus a kill-fed siege arsenal.*

### 8. Reverse Engineering
- Salvaging an item refunds its **full component materials**. Each salvage grants **+5% craft speed for 1 minute** (stacks).
- *Lossless recycling that makes your next crafts faster.*

### 9. The Inventor
- **Once per match**, craft **any item instantly and free** (bypasses materials and table — may be crafted at **Ancient** quality, Genius+ gate). Crafting it grants the carrier **+5% Max HP**.
- *The ultimate flex craft — a perfect item, with a bonus for the one who wields it.*

### 10. Golden Bazaar
- Bazaar pity prices **−40%**; you may buy **1 extra material** per turn. **Every purchase** makes the next purchase **10% cheaper** (stacking).
- *The Bazaar becomes a real supply line that discounts itself the more you use it.*
