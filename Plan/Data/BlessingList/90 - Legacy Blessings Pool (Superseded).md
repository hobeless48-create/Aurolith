## Part 1 — 📜 Trait Draft Mechanics

> [!WARNING] SUPERSEDED
> This 15-trait draft system is **replaced** by the 150-trait tiered system in **`Data/TraitList/00 - Trait System Overview, Draft Rules & Audit.md`** (5 tiers × 30 traits, mirrored tiers, 3 panels of 4 options, 1 reroll per panel). Kept for history and as source material — the trait names below survive as the upgraded forms at Perfect tier in the new list.

> [!NOTE]
> The Trait Draft takes place during the untimed **Prep Phase** (~90s) before the match clock starts, allowing Commanders to customize their faction's strategic path.

---

### 1. Draft Rules & Mechanics

- **Timing:** Prep Phase only.
- **Rounds:** **3 Draft Rounds Total**.
- **Pool Generation:** Each round offers **5 randomized traits** pulled from a master pool of 15 traits across 3 categories (*Combat*, *Economy*, *Item*). The 5 options are always **unique — no trait can appear twice within a round**, and a drafted trait is removed from subsequent rounds.
- **Selection:** Commander picks **1 trait per round**.
- **Maximum Limit:** **3 Traits Maximum** per player.
- **Random Distribution Rule:** The pool does **NOT** guarantee one trait from each of the 3 categories per round. A player could end up drafting 2 Combat traits and 1 Economy trait, or 3 Item traits. This randomness provides match-to-match setup variance.

## Part 2 — ⚔️ Combat Traits

> [!NOTE]
> Combat traits directly boost the stats, damage, armor, and combat effectiveness of friendly units and platoons.

---

### 📜 The 5 Combat Traits

#### 1. Vanguard Strike
- **Effect:** Increases base physical damage of all units by **+20%**. When a platoon makes its first attack against an unbreached enemy wall segment, it gains an additional **+15% siege bonus damage for 10 seconds** (trigger once per platoon per segment; overlapping refreshes do not stack).

#### 2. Iron Armor
- **Effect:** Grants **+25 Armor** and **+50 Max HP** to all Gilded Infantry and Siege Operator units.

#### 3. Golden Fervor
- **Effect:** Every unit kill grants the attacking platoon **+5% Attack Speed** for 8 seconds (stacks up to 5 times to +25%).

#### 4. Bloodlust
- **Effect:** Grants **15% Melee Lifesteal** on all physical attacks. When a unit falls below 30% HP, its attack speed doubles for 5 seconds.

#### 5. Precision Aim
- **Effect:** Increases attack range of Arbalest Marksmen by **+1 Hex** and grants a 20% Critical Strike chance (`1.8x Damage`) on ranged attacks.

## Part 3 — 🪙 Economy Traits

> [!NOTE]
> Economy traits accelerate resource generation, unit recruitment speeds, and tech level-up rates.

---

### 📜 The 5 Economy Traits

#### 1. Gilded Taxes
- **Effect:** Increases passive Gold generation by **+25%**. Gain an instant bonus of `100 Gold` upon breaching an enemy Outer Wall.

#### 2. Rapid Bounty
- **Effect:** Increases Gold and XP bounties earned from clearing Jungle Neutral Camps by **+40%**.

#### 3. Scholar's Blessing
- **Effect:** Increases passive XP generation by **+30%**, reducing all Inner Wall Facility tier upgrade costs by 20%.

#### 4. Merchant Discount
- **Effect:** Reduces initial unit recruitment costs by **20%** and caps Card Buy scaling cost escalation to a maximum of `150 Gold`.

#### 5. Prospector's Luck
- **Effect:** Every 2 minutes, automatically grants `150 Free Gold` and a 15% chance to obtain a free Card Draw ticket.

## Part 4 — 🧪 Item Traits

> [!NOTE]
> Item traits grant instant alchemical consumables, reduce item production costs, or amplify item effectiveness on platoons.
> **Item System Reference:** See `Data/ItemList/00 - Item List, Acquisition & Crafting.md` **§9 — Legacy Consumables Lane** for item slots, crafting rules, and consumable definitions.

---

### 📜 The 5 Item Traits

#### 1. Alchemical Cache
- **Effect:** Instantly grants **3 Free Healing Flasks** and **1 Siege Bomb** at match start.

#### 2. Master Smith
- **Effect:** Increases stat boosts granted by equipped items on platoons by **+35%**.

#### 3. Elixir Mastery
- **Effect:** Doubles the duration of all consumable stat potions (e.g. Speed Elixirs, Damage Tonics) used on platoons.

#### 4. Relic Affinity
- **Effect:** Unlocks an extra Item Slot (2 slots total) on all Platoon Commanders.

#### 5. Quick Crafting
- **Effect:** Reduces item creation crafting time by **50%** and cuts crafting Gold cost by 25%.
